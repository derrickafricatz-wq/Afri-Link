SPIRITUAL GUIDE

1. Buying A Book
Open the Book Store and choose the book you want to read.

2. Payment
Make payment using the payment option shown under the selected book package.

3. Payment Verification
After payment, press VERIFY PAYMENT and confirm the name used during payment.

4. Receiving Your License Key
Once your payment is verified, you will receive a license key.

5. Unlocking A Book
Press UNLOCK, enter your license key, then press UNLOCK BOOK.

6. Reading Your Book
After unlocking, the book opens instantly and is added to your library for future access.

7. Next & Previous Navigation
While reading, use NEXT and PREV buttons to move between pages easily.

8. Rekindle View
If the reader freezes, shows blank pages, or loads incorrectly, press ⚡ REKINDLE VIEW to restore the current reading session without losing progress.

9. Author Portal
Authors must create an account using their name, email, WhatsApp phone number, and password. After registration, they can log in anytime using email and password to view their book data and performance.
